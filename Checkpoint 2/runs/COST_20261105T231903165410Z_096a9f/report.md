## Overview

Costco operates membership‑driven warehouses and e‑commerce sites built around the premise that low prices on a limited assortment of national‑brand and private‑label products generate high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover, which in turn supports profitability at lower gross margins than many peers [source:K:L129]. The company emphasizes employee stability, noting a high retention rate and a strategic focus on maintaining at least half of its workforce as full‑time employees, which it views as a competitive advantage [source:K:L191]. ## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco leverages its scale to negotiate favorable terms with suppliers, reinforcing a perception of pricing authority among members and allowing the firm to pass savings through to shoppers [source:Q:L651]. Its self‑service warehouse design and efficient distribution network contribute to productivity that exceeds many competitors [source:K:L129]. The balance sheet remains modestly leveraged, with a low debt‑to‑equity profile [[COST.ratio.debt_to_equity]], and the company generates strong free cash flow [[COST.ratio.free_cash_flow]], providing flexibility for reinvestment and shareholder returns. Growth is pursued through expansion in existing markets and entry into new ones, though the firm cautions that unsuccessful execution of this strategy could adversely affect results [source:K:L233].
