## Overview

Visa operates a global payments network that connects consumers, issuers, acquirers and merchants through a four‑party model, providing authorization, clearing and settlement services. The company does not issue cards or extend credit, focusing instead on secure, reliable and efficient money movement. It offers both Visa‑branded and non‑Visa‑branded transaction processing, earning service and data‑processing revenue depending on the client’s use of its network. ## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Visa’s growth strategy centers on expanding consumer payments, commercial and money‑movement solutions, and value‑added services.
