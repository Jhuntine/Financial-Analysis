## Overview

Costco operates membership‑based warehouses and e‑commerce platforms that focus on offering low prices on a limited assortment of nationally‑branded and private‑label items. The model relies on high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover, which together enable profitability despite lower gross margins. The company emphasizes efficient distribution, volume purchasing and a self‑service warehouse format to keep costs down. Employee retention is highlighted as a strength, with a focus on competitive compensation, benefits and career development contributing to a high retention rate among full‑time staff. Strong operating cash generation is reflected in the cash flow metric [[COST.fact.operating_cash_flow]], supporting ongoing investments and shareholder returns.
