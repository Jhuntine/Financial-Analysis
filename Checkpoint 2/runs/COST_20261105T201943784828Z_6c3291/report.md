## Overview

Costco operates membership‑based warehouses and e‑commerce sites that focus on offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally‑branded and private‑label products, driving high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover while maintaining lower gross margins than many peers. [source:K:L129] The company emphasizes efficient distribution, volume purchasing and self‑service facilities to keep costs down. Employee retention is a strategic priority, with competitive compensation, benefits and career development contributing to a high retention rate among full‑time staff. [source:K:L191] Costco acknowledges execution risk around its growth strategy, including market expansion and acquisition integration, which could adversely affect results. [source:K:L233] Increasing cyber threats and data‑privacy obligations pose operational and reputational risks. [source:K:L252] Ongoing litigation and regulatory matters add further uncertainty. [source:Q:L508]

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook
