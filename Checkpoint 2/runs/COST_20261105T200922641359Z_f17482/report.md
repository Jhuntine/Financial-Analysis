## Overview

Costco operates membership warehouses and e‑commerce sites that focus on low prices for a limited assortment of national and private‑label products, driving high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover. This model enables the company to profit at lower gross margins than many peers and to capture early‑payment discounts by often selling inventory before payment is due. [[source:K:L129]]
