## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Visa operates a global payments network that connects consumers, merchants, issuers and acquirers to enable secure, reliable and efficient money movement. The company does not issue cards or extend credit, but provides authorization, clearing and settlement services through its four‑party model. [source:K:L151] The core platform, VisaNet, serves as a single connection point for a wide range of transaction types across more than two hundred countries and territories. [source:Q:L1253] The firm’s strategy focuses on expanding consumer payments, commercial and money‑movement solutions, and value‑added services while strengthening foundational capabilities. [source:K:L214] Investment priorities include token technology, cross‑border capabilities and new digital commerce tools to broaden transaction volume and scope. [source:K:L220]
